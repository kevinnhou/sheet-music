> Projectile Motion refers to the curved trajectory an object follows when it is thrown near the Earth's surface, moving under the influence of gravity alone.

## Assumptions

- No Air Resistance
- Constant Vertical Acceleration due to gravity

Projectile Motion can be resolved into the rectilinear motion of the horizontal and vertical components.

## Horizontal Motion

Rectilinear motion with constant velocity.

export const horizontalSuvat = `
\\begin{alignedat}{2}
   v_x &= u_x \\\\
   s_x &= u_x t
\\end{alignedat}
`

## Vertical Motion

Rectilinear motion with constant acceleration due to gravity.

export const verticalSuvat = `
\\begin{alignedat}{3}
   v_y &= u_x - gt \\\\
   s_y &= u_x t - \\frac{1}{2} g t^2 \\\\
   v_y^2 &= u_x^2 - 2g s_y
\\end{alignedat}
`
