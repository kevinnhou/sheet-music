## SUVAT

Set of equations that describe the motion of an object under constant acceleration.


### Derivation

Consider an object moving in a straight line with constant acceleration $a$:

- Let $u$ be the initial velocity (at time $t = 0$).
- Let $v$ be the final velocity after time $t$.

By definition, acceleration is the rate of change of velocity:

$$
\frac{v - u}{t} = a
$$

Rearrange to solve for $v$:

$$
\begin{equation}
  \begin{split}
    v - u &= at \\
    v &= u + at
  \end{split}
\end{equation}
$$

$ \because $ constant acceleration, average velocity, $\bar{v}$ is:

$$
\bar{v} = \frac{u + v}{2}
$$

The displacement $s$ after time $t$ is:

$$
s = \bar{v} t = \frac{u + v}{2} t
$$

Substitute $v = u + at$ into the above to solve for $s$:

$$
\begin{equation}
  \begin{split}
    s &= \frac{u + (u + at)}{2} t \\
      &= \frac{2*u + at}{2} t \\
      &= ut + \frac{1}{2} a t^2
  \end{split}
\end{equation}
$$

Alternatively, to eliminate $t$, start with $v = u + at$ and rearrange for $t$:

$$
t = \frac{v - u}{a}
$$

Substitute this into $s = \frac{u + v}{2} t$:

$$
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
s &= \frac{u + v}{2} \cdot \frac{v - u}{a} \\
  &= \frac{(u + v)(v - u)}{2*a} \\
  &= \frac{v^2 - u^2}{2*a}
\end{split}
\end{equation}
$$

Rearrange to get:

$$
v^2 = u^2 + 2as
$$

export const SUVAT = `
\\begin{alignedat}{3}
   v &= u + at \\\\
   s &= ut + \\frac{1}{2} a t^2 \\\\
   v^2 &= u^2 + 2as
\\end{alignedat}
`

### Initial Velocity

Given angle $\theta$ and initial velocity $u$, the initial velocity can be resolved into horizontal and vertical components [^1]:

$$
\begin{alignedat}{2}
   u_x &= u \cos\theta \quad &&\text{(horizontal component)} \\
   u_y &= u \sin\theta \quad &&\text{(vertical component)}
\end{alignedat}
$$

### Maximum Height

Reached at the apex where vertical velocity is $0$. 

$$ \therefore u \sin\theta - gt = 0 $$ where $$ t = \frac{u \sin\theta}{g} $$

Substitute this into vertical motion:

$$
s = u \sin\theta \cdot \frac{u \sin\theta}{g} - \frac{1}{2} g \left(\frac{u \sin\theta}{g}\right)^2
$$

$$
s = \frac{u^2 \sin^2\theta}{2g}
$$

### Time of Flight

Since Projectile motion is symmetrical, the time of flight is twice the time to reach the maximum height.

$$
t = \frac{2u \sin\theta}{g}
$$

### Range

Substitute the time of flight into horizontal motion:

$$
\begin{alignedat}{3}
  s &= u \cos\theta \cdot \frac{2u \sin\theta}{g} \\
    &= \frac{2u^2 \sin\theta \cos\theta}{g} \\
    &= \frac{u^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}
\end{alignedat}
$$
